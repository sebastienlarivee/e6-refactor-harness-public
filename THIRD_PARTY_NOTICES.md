## Bundled benchmark material

The original reference declarations and metadata in `data/benchmark_data_final.jsonl`, `data/benchmark_data_warmup.jsonl`, `data/tasks/`, and the test fixtures were obtained from the [Lean Refactor Arena](https://huggingface.co/spaces/delta-lab-ai/lean-refactor-arena). The final input was downloaded on November 1, 2026 from application revision `da5f65085398d1d0ca7838fd56da3a9ec778af42` (SHA-256 `d53690b057f9972f44643678ee6b5eee7a66f56e1a7d2678a2e5ff47fadbd682`). Warmup provenance is recorded in `data/benchmark-provenance.json`.

Proof declarations were extracted into JSON; warmup task records additionally contain normalized signature/context fields. The supplied original proof text is retained. Measurements and baseline/control metadata were generated by the harness. Project configuration excerpts are retained in `environment-sources/environment-setup.json`.

## Separately installed dependencies

Lean, Mathlib, other Lean packages, Codex CLI, and Python dependencies are fetched or installed during setup. Their source trees and binaries are not bundled in this repository; their upstream licenses and notices accompany those installations. Exact project/package commits and runtime pins are recorded in the environment manifest, lockfile, and setup scripts.

The harness's extraction and development checkpoints are recorded in `docs/provenance.json`. No endorsement by the benchmark organizers or upstream authors is implied.
